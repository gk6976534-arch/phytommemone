Phytomem One Ingredients

Phytomem One™ combines a selection of botanical extracts and nutritional compounds designed to complement a daily wellness routine. Here are 8 key ingredients commonly highlighted in the formula:

1

Saffron Extract

Saffron is a botanical ingredient traditionally valued for its wellness properties. It contains naturally occurring compounds such as crocin and safranal and has been studied for its potential role in supporting mood, mental wellness, and cognitive function.
2

Fucoxanthin

Fucoxanthin is a naturally occurring carotenoid found in brown seaweed and other marine algae. It provides antioxidant properties and has been investigated for its potential role in supporting healthy cellular and metabolic functions.
3

Fucoidan

Fucoidan is a complex polysaccharide naturally found in certain species of brown seaweed. It has attracted scientific interest for its antioxidant and general wellness properties, making it a complementary ingredient in a plant-based nutritional formula.
4

Olive Leaf Extract

Olive leaf extract comes from the leaves of the olive tree and contains naturally occurring polyphenols, particularly oleuropein. These compounds are known for their antioxidant properties and may help support healthy cellular function and overall wellness.
5

Berberine

Berberine is a naturally occurring plant compound found in several traditional medicinal plants. It has been extensively studied for its relationship with healthy metabolic processes, glucose metabolism, and overall cellular wellness.
6

Ceylon Cinnamon

Ceylon cinnamon is a traditional botanical known for its distinctive flavor and naturally occurring polyphenols. It is commonly included in wellness formulas for its antioxidant properties and potential support for healthy metabolic balance.
7

Corosolic Acid

Corosolic acid is a naturally occurring compound found in banaba leaves. Research has explored its potential connection with healthy glucose metabolism and other aspects of metabolic wellness, making it a useful addition to a broader nutritional formula.
8

Morosil®

Morosil® is a branded extract derived from Moro blood oranges. It naturally contains citrus polyphenols and anthocyanins, which provide antioxidant compounds and complement the other botanical ingredients in the formula.
